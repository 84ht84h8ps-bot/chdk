Infinite Focus Stacking with focus_stack.lua

Complete step-by-step tutorial: automated EF-M lens focus racking and depth-of-field blending in Photoshop

The Optics: Why f/8 Stacking Beats f/22

Landscape photographers often stop down to f/22 to get everything in focus, but the resulting image is soft and blurry across the entire frame. This is caused by optical diffraction: on the EOS M3's tiny 3.72-micron pixels, light waves passing through a tiny f/22 pinhole bend and smear fine details across adjacent pixels.

By shooting multiple frames at the lens's optical sweet spot (f/8) while stepping the focus motor incrementally from foreground to infinity, you get infinite depth of field with 3× higher optical sharpness!

Step 1: Mandatory EOS M3 Settings & Running focus_stack.lua

⚠️
3 Required Settings on the Canon EOS M3 Before Focus Stacking

Unlike PowerShot compacts, the EOS M3 does not support CHDK's standard set_focus() override. It drives electronic Canon EF-M lenses directly via Canon's internal FA.Create / EFLensCom.MoveFocus firmware routines. You must set these 3 options once:

  1. Enable Lua Native Calls in CHDK: Press M-Fn (<ALT>) → MENU → Miscellaneous stuff → check Enable Lua Native Calls [ • ] (press SET on "Are you sure?").
  2. Set Canon Focus Mode to MF: EF-M lenses have no physical AF/MF switch on the barrel. Exit <ALT> mode, press Q / SET → set Focus Mode to MF (Manual Focus), and in the Canon Menu set Continuous AF: Off (otherwise half-pressing the shutter re-autofocuses every shot!).
  3. Use an Electronic EF-M (or Adapted EF/EF-S) Lens: Manual-only third-party lenses (e.g. TTArtisan/7Artisans) have no internal stepper motor.
  1. Mount your camera on a steady tripod. Set mode dial to M or Av at f/8, ISO 100.
  2. In Canon MF mode, turn the focus ring to focus sharply on the closest object in your foreground (or set Start Position = 1 (Near/MFD) to let the script drive the lens to Minimum Focus Distance automatically).
  3. Press M-Fn (<ALT>) → MENU → Script → Load Script from File... → select focus_stack.lua.
  4. Verify script parameters:
    • Start Position (0=Current, 1=Near/MFD): 0 (starts from your current foreground focus) or 1 (drives to MFD first)
    • Max Shots (Safety Cap): 15
    • Initial Step Size (Motor Steps): 25
    • Step Growth % per Shot: 40 (progressively widens motor steps as depth of field expands with distance)
    • Final True-Infinity Shot: 1 (Yes) (drives lens to optical infinity via EFLensCom.FocusSearchFar for the final frame)
    • Initial Delay: 2 (seconds)
  5. Press MENU to exit. Press the Shutter button.
  6. Automated Execution:
    • The script initializes FA.Create & InitializeAdjustmentSystem, forces set_mf(1), locks exposure (Tv/Av), and captures your close foreground.
    • On each step, it drives the EF-M lens outward via EFLensCom.MoveFocus with +40% progressive step growth, displaying live motor step position and Canon focus distance (e.g. 25cm → 60cm → 1.8m → INF) on screen.
    • As soon as Canon reports optical infinity (81m+) or the motor hits the infinity stop, it stops early so no duplicate frames are wasted, and finishes with a guaranteed True Infinity frame via EFLensCom.FocusSearchFar!

Step 2: Blending the Focus Slices in Photoshop

Because focusing from close to far slightly alters the optical focal length (known as focus breathing), stacking software must automatically align and scale the layers before blending:

Focus Stacking Gotchas & Troubleshooting

Issue Cause Solution
Lens motor does not step between shots Lua Native Calls disabled in CHDK In CHDK Menu → Miscellaneous stuff → check Enable Lua Native Calls [ * ].
Blurry patches appear in final blend Step size was too large, leaving unsharp focal gaps Increase Number of Shots to 15 or reduce Step Size to 10 for deep macro scenes.
Ghosting around foliage Wind moved leaves between frames Shoot on calm days or use Photoshop's Layer Mask brush to paint in the sharpest single frame for moving foliage.
