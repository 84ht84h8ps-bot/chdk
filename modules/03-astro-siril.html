60-Shot Astrophotography & Siril Stacking

Complete field and post-processing tutorial: shooting with fixedint.lua and processing in Siril & Lightroom

Step 1: Calculate Your Max Exposure (500 Rule)

To prevent stars from turning into blurry streaks due to Earth's rotation, use the 500 Rule tailored to the EOS M3's 1.6× crop factor:

Max_Seconds = 500 / (Focal_Length × 1.6)

Lens / Focal Length Calculation Safe Shutter Speed
EF-M 11-22mm @ 11mm 500 / (11 × 1.6) = 28.4s 20s to 25s
EF-M 15-45mm @ 15mm 500 / (15 × 1.6) = 20.8s 15s to 20s
EF-M 22mm f/2 STM 500 / (22 × 1.6) = 14.2s 13s to 15s

Step 2: Field Focusing with 10× Live View Zoom

Autofocus cannot see in the dark, and lens infinity marks are physically inaccurate. Focus using this exact technique:

1

Switch to MF

Set lens to MF (Manual Focus). Aperture wide open (e.g. f/2, f/3.5). ISO to 3200.

2

Zoom 10× on Bright Star

Center the brightest star in the sky on your LCD. Press the blue Magnify button on the back twice for 10× zoom.

3

Dial Down to Tiny Pinpoint

Turn the focus ring slowly. The star will shrink from a blurry blob into a tiny, sharp dot. When it is as small as possible, stop.

4

Tape the Focus Ring

Apply a strip of tape across the focus ring so your hands don't accidentally bump it during the 60-shot sequence.

Step 3: Configuring fixedint.lua in CHDK

  1. Press M-Fn (<ALT>) → MENU → Script → Load Script from File... → choose fixedint.lua.
  2. Set Shots (0=unlimited) to 60 (about 15-20 minutes total capture).
  3. Set Interval Sec/10 to 0 (captures back-to-back with zero dead-time).
  4. Set Tv to 15 or 20 (matching your 500-rule calculation).
  5. Set Disable Canon Dark Frame to true (CRUCIAL: prevents 15s freeze between shots).
  6. Set Display to Blt_Off (kills LCD backlight: cuts sensor thermal noise and saves battery).
  7. Set Start delay (ms) to 3000 (3 seconds for tripod vibrations to settle).
  8. Set Shutdown on low battery to true (safely flushes SD card if battery dies).
  9. Press MENU to close. Press the Shutter button to start the 60-shot run!

Step 5: Final Creative Polish in Lightroom

Import your 16-bit TIFF output from Siril into Lightroom:

  • Dehaze (+10 to +20): Strips away lingering atmospheric haze and makes the Milky Way pop.
  • Clarity (+15) & Texture (+10): Accentuates dust lanes and dark nebula structures.
  • Noise Reduction → Luminance: Set to 0! Because you stacked 60 frames in Siril, the background noise is already mathematically eliminated.
  • Radial Mask: Draw an oval over the galactic core, boost Exposure by +0.3 and Warmth by +5.
